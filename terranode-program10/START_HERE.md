# TerraNode Program 10

Estimated time: 10-15 minutes

## Goal

Evaluate whether TerraNode makes it easier to understand coordination than ordinary logs.

## Steps

1. Read PROGRAM10.md.
2. Run the demo.
3. Compare the "ordinary logs" view with the TerraNode view.
4. Answer the questionnaire.
5. Open a GitHub Issue titled "Program 10 Feedback" and answer:

- What confused you?
- What took the longest?
- What information was easiest to find?
- What information was hardest to find?
- Would you use something like this? Why or why not?
- Any bugs or suggestions?
