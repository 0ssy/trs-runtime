# TRS Ecosystem Research Charter

## Core conclusion from completed TRS research

The TRS research program is complete enough to freeze ontology at three primitives:

- Observation
- Commitment
- Intention

Current work is ecosystem validation, not primitive expansion.

## Overarching falsification question

Can independent systems evolve for years on top of the frozen three-primitive kernel without requiring a fourth primitive?

## Era structure

### Era I — Prove TerraNode as a Consumer (Programs 2–9)

Objective: demonstrate TerraNode behavior evolves in policies, adapters, and UX while `runtime/` remains semantically frozen.

Exit condition: Programs 2–9 complete with TRS surviving and no ontology changes.

### Era II — Prove TRS as a Standard (Programs 9.5–9.11)

Objective: prove portability, formal soundness, production security posture, governance, adversarial resilience, onboarding viability, and privacy extensions.

Exit condition: Programs 9.5–9.11 close with evidence-linked pass criteria.

### Era III — Ecosystem Expansion

Objective: onboard additional independent consumers (beyond TerraNode) with domain payloads and adapters only.

Exit condition: multiple external systems adopt TRS without requiring a fourth primitive.

## Hard boundary

- Ontology and normative TRS semantics remain frozen unless a reproducible contradiction triggers an accepted amendment.
- Domain behavior belongs in payloads, well-formedness, policies, adapters, governance, and ecosystem tooling.

## Outcome rule for every program/cycle

Exactly one:

1. TRS survives
2. Reproducible contradiction -> amendment candidate
